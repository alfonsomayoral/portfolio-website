About me

Engineer by training, builder by instinct, student forever.

I’m an engineer who loves building things. I’ve been taking computers apart since I was a kid, fascinated by how they worked, and I never really stopped. Today I work as a Junior AI Engineer at Suntory Global Spirits, shipping enterprise AI to teams that depend on it daily. I’m finishing my last year of Telecommunications Engineering at UC3M, after a year on exchange in California that ended on the Chancellor’s Honor List. At night and on weekends I build my own projects — that’s where most of my best work starts.

One of those side projects became Spotter AI. It started from something I kept noticing: people juggling five or six health and fitness apps, paying a premium subscription for each one, and still ending up with shallow tracking and features that felt thin. I wanted a single, well-designed app that did the heavy lifting — so I built one. Spotter AI brings workouts, nutrition and fitness analysis into one mobile experience, powered by AI. It now reaches 1,000+ downloads and 400+ weekly active users across iOS and Android.

Around the same time, a few friends and I noticed something else: at university, the people most excited about AI and shipping real things didn’t really have a place to find each other. So we started AISC Madrid — the first European chapter of the AI Student Collective. The idea was to give back what we’d been learning over the years: demystify the buzzword “AI”, teach its fundamentals, share the practical tools that actually move the needle today, and bring in speakers from the companies quietly changing the world.

I move fast across AI, full-stack and cloud — but speed isn’t the point. What I actually care about is shipping things that matter, working with people I respect, and ending every week knowing a little more than I did at the start of it.
